WordPress websites

I design websites in Figma and build them as custom WordPress themes. Six of the eleven projects on this site were made this way, for a hotel and its restaurant, a construction company, a tile showroom, a windows and doors installer, a home-care provider and a marketing agency.

What you get

  • A design before a build

    Every page is drawn before it exists, with your own text and pictures in it, so you see the site before it is built.

  • A theme of your own

    Built around what you actually sell, not adapted from a bought template. The structure follows the work, not the other way round.

  • Yours to edit

    Gutenberg or Elementor where you need to change words and pictures yourself, without breaking the design.

  • Written to be found

    Titles, descriptions, structured data and image text written page by page, rather than left to a plugin to guess.

  • Fast and reachable

    Modern image formats, images loaded as they are needed, contrast and keyboard access checked before launch.

  • A shop, where a shop is needed

    WooCommerce: products, checkout, payments, and the admin side the people behind the counter will actually use.

How it goes

  • Understand

    I ask what people come to the site for, and where they give up. For the Cityhotel I sat with reception and asked which questions guests really ask.

  • Design

    The real pages in Figma, with your content in them, until the thing reads the way it should.

  • Build

    A custom theme, put together page by page and tested on real devices, not only in a browser window.

  • Launch and hand over

    You get the Figma file and the code, so nothing about your own site is locked away from you.
