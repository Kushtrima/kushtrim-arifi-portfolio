About

I look at digital product development through the lens of human behavior. With over 10 years of experience spanning UX research, product design, UX architecture, and front-end development, my work focuses on understanding how people think, make decisions, and interact with digital systems. I have delivered over 60 websites, applications, and digital projects, including more than seven years working remotely with Swiss companies.

My background in Clinical Psychology brings an additional perspective to understanding human cognition, behavior, and decision-making throughout the design process. I investigate how users navigate information, interpret choices, and experience cognitive friction through user research, stakeholder interviews, journey mapping, and usability testing. I translate these insights into clear information architectures, intuitive interaction patterns, Figma prototypes, and reusable design systems that simplify complex digital experiences.

Beyond design, I bring hands-on engineering experience that allows me to translate product decisions into working solutions. Using React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Supabase, I develop production-ready interfaces while maintaining consistency between research findings, design intentions, and technical implementation. This combination allows me to work across the entire product lifecycle, connecting human understanding with functional digital products.
