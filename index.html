(About me)

Started as a designer in 2012, moved through UX/UI, then into software engineering in 2020. Since then I've worked across websites, mobile apps and custom CRM systems, most recently an AI mental health tool built with a clinical team.

My MSc in Clinical Psychology means I run my own user research (interviews, usability testing, measurement) rather than designing from assumptions. I enjoy solving real problems and building things that make sense to the user, not just the screen.
