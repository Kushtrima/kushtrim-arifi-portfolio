About Kushtrim Arifi

I’m Kushtrim, a product designer based in Kosovo. I design websites and digital products, with a focus on usability and interaction design. I aim to reduce cognitive load through clear navigation, a strong visual hierarchy, and consistent interactions, helping people find what they need and understand what to do next.

I started in graphic design and branding in 2012. Over time, my work grew into UX/UI and product design. Since 2021, I’ve worked almost exclusively with Swiss companies, alongside occasional projects in Kosovo.

After completing two full-stack bootcamps, I also began working as a developer. I work mainly on the front end, and also handle back-end development. I enjoy being able to build what I design and work through the details as it takes shape.

I also have a master’s degree in Clinical Psychology. Studying people’s behaviour has shaped how I approach user research and usability testing. I pay attention to where someone hesitates, what they expect to happen, and what makes a task confusing. Those observations help me question my assumptions and refine the design.

Recently, I’ve been working on an AI mental health support tool with a clinical team—a project that draws on my experience in design, development, and psychology.

AI

I use AI to amplify my own thinking, not to replace it. I don’t use its speed to cut corners. I use it as a tool: to explore more directions early, to question my assumptions, and to spend the time it saves on the details that decide whether a product feels right. The judgement of what to build, what to leave out and why stays mine.
